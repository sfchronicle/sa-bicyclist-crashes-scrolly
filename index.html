About the data

The analysis uses Texas Department of Transportation CRIS records for people identified as pedalcyclists with a fatal injury or suspected serious injury in San Antonio crashes. Two qualifying crashes had no usable coordinates and therefore are included in the totals but not shown as map points.

The candidate stretches are an exploratory analysis of repeat crashes matched to local street segments. They are not an official City of San Antonio ranking or designation.
